# 2026-09-11

The first board to be designed for RoverSpine was defined: a small passive
adapter replacing the loose dupont control wires between the Raspberry Pi 5 and
the WHEELTEC D50A motor driver. This session produced a plan, not a schematic.

> Two statements in this entry were corrected on [2026-09-12](2026-09-12.md):
> the board carries **ten** connections, not seven, and the D50A's `V` pins
> **must** be connected to 3.3 V.

## Rev A scope

- Two connectors, direct copper connections, readable silkscreen.
- No microcontroller, no firmware.
- No battery input, no voltage regulator, no motor current, no motor power.

Future power, sensing, safety and motor-driver boards are separate projects and
will not be added to Rev A.

## Physical arrangement

The Pi 5 carries an active cooler, so nothing can stack on its 40-pin header. The
adapter sits in a small enclosure on the upper deck, fixed with double-sided
tape, with a ribbon running down to the driver on the lower deck.

```
Raspberry Pi 5
    │  2×20 ribbon, female-to-female, 10–15 cm
    ▼
RoverSpine passive adapter (upper deck, in an enclosure)
    │  2×5 IDC ribbon, female-to-female, ≈35 cm
    ▼
WHEELTEC D50A (lower deck)
```

## Signal map

| Function | BCM | Pi physical pin |
|---|---:|---:|
| PWM1 | GPIO12 | 32 |
| INA1 | GPIO23 | 16 |
| INB1 | GPIO24 | 18 |
| PWM2 | GPIO13 | 33 |
| INA2 | GPIO5 | 29 |
| INB2 | GPIO6 | 31 |
| GND | GND | 39 |

The D50A control header measures slightly over 11 mm across five positions,
consistent with 2.54 mm pitch. Footprint and pin-1 orientation are not frozen
until the delivered cables are checked.

## Working method

Andy draws the board in KiCad step by step. The assistant explains each decision,
checks the result, and helps diagnose errors rather than completing the design.

## Next

1. Create the KiCad project for Rev A.
2. Place the 2×20 and 2×5 connectors.
3. Draw and label the connections.
4. Run ERC and compare every net against the signal map.
5. When the cables arrive, verify pitch, key direction, pin 1, body size and
   clearance.
6. Only then assign footprints and begin layout.

# 2026-09-11 / 中文

确定了 RoverSpine 第一块准备实际设计的 PCB：一块小型被动转接板，用来替换 Raspberry Pi 5
与 WHEELTEC D50A 电机驱动板之间松散的杜邦控制线。本次产出的是计划，不是原理图。

> 本篇有两处陈述已于 [2026-09-12](2026-09-12.md) 更正：这块板是**十个**连接不是七个，
> 而且 D50A 的 `V` 脚**必须**接 3.3 V。

## Rev A 范围

- 两个连接器、铜箔直连、清楚的丝印。
- 不用单片机，不需要固件。
- 不接入电池、不做电源转换、不碰电机电流和功率。

以后可能制作的电源、传感、安全控制和电机驱动板是独立项目，不会并入 Rev A。

## 机械安装方案

树莓派 5 上装了主动散热器，40 针排针上不能再叠任何东西。转接板装在小外壳里，用双面胶固定
在第二层，再用排线绕到第一层的驱动板。

```
Raspberry Pi 5
    │  2×20 排线，母对母，10–15 cm
    ▼
RoverSpine 被动转接板（第二层，装在外壳里）
    │  2×5 IDC 排线，母对母，约 35 cm
    ▼
WHEELTEC D50A（第一层）
```

## 信号表

| 功能 | BCM | Pi 物理针脚 |
|---|---:|---:|
| PWM1 | GPIO12 | 32 |
| INA1 | GPIO23 | 16 |
| INB1 | GPIO24 | 18 |
| PWM2 | GPIO13 | 33 |
| INA2 | GPIO5 | 29 |
| INB2 | GPIO6 | 31 |
| GND | GND | 39 |

D50A 控制口五个位置的实测跨距略大于 11 mm，与 2.54 mm 间距相符。封装和 1 号针方向要等排线
到货实测之后才锁定。

## 工作方式

板子由 Andy 在 KiCad 中一步一步亲自绘制。助手负责解释每个决定、检查结果、帮助排错，
不代替完成设计。

## 下一步

1. 建立 Rev A 的 KiCad 工程。
2. 放置 2×20 和 2×5 连接器。
3. 画出并标注全部连接。
4. 跑 ERC，并把每条网络与信号表逐条比对。
5. 排线到货后，核对间距、防呆方向、1 号针、连接器本体尺寸和间隙。
6. 之后才分配封装、开始布局。
