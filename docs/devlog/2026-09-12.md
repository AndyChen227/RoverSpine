## D50A control header — two corrections

**1. `V` must be connected, at 3.3 V.** The records had specified leaving the
D50A's two `V` pins unconnected. `V` is the supply for the driver's isolated
control side; without it none of the six control signals work. `+3V3` is sourced
only from Pi physical pins 1 and 17. **5 V would destroy the isolated input.**

**2. The board carries 10 connections, not 7.** The earlier signal map omitted
both `V` pins and the second `G`.

| D50A silkscreen | Signal | Pi physical pin | BCM / rail | Channel |
|:---:|---|---:|---|---|
| `P1` | `PWM1` | 32 | GPIO12 | 1 — left |
| `A1` | `INA1` | 16 | GPIO23 | 1 |
| `B1` | `INB1` | 18 | GPIO24 | 1 |
| `P2` | `PWM2` | 33 | GPIO13 | 2 — right |
| `A2` | `INA2` | 29 | GPIO5 | 2 |
| `B2` | `INB2` | 31 | GPIO6 | 2 |
| `G` (×2) | `GND` | 34, 39 | GND | both |
| `V` (×2) | `+3V3` | **1, 17** | **3.3 V — never 5 V** | both |

The board's silkscreen is the short form `V P1 A1 B1 G`, not the datasheet's
`VCC PWM1 INA1 INB1 GND`. The control header is a boxed header, so both ends of
the ribbon can be keyed.

**Pin 1 is unresolved.** The photographs do not show the shroud's key notch or a
square pad. Getting it wrong puts `PWM1` on the driver's `P2` input. This does not
block drawing, but it is a hard gate before footprints are frozen: plug the keyed
ribbon onto the D50A, then find by continuity which conductor reaches each signal.
`G` is continuous with the motor-power negative `P-`, which cross-checks the map.

**Direction truth table**, from WHEELTEC's own STM32 example (`moto.c` v5.7):

| Action | `A` | `B` | PWM duty |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| Forward | high | low | 3000/7200 ≈ 42% |
| Reverse | low | high | 4000/7200 ≈ 56% |

PWM runs at 10 kHz. The vendor never drives both direction pins to the same
level, so "both low means stopped" is an assumption until measured.

## Rev A schematic drawn and verified

J1 (2×20) and J2 (2×5) were drawn, connected by same-named labels rather than
wires across the sheet. ERC: **30 errors / 0 warnings**, all 30 being unused pins
on the 2×20 header.

Every net was then compared against the signal map as a string comparison rather
than by reading. **ERC cannot catch a swap** — if `PWM1` and `PWM2` were placed on
each other's pins, each net still has two pins and nothing dangles, so ERC reports
nothing and the motors run reversed.

## Twelve resistors were missing from the drawing

The roadmap and BOM specified 6 series resistors and 6 pull-downs. **They were
never drawn.** Adding them splits each signal into a Pi-side and a driver-side
net, so Rev A is **14 nets, not 8**.

- **The series resistors changed from 0805 to through-hole, and are mandatory.**
  They sit in the signal path, so an empty series pad is an open signal.
- **Driver-side nets are named after the D50A's own silkscreen** — `P1`, `A1`,
  `B1` — not `PWM1_D`. The label on the net becomes the label on the board.
- **The pull-down goes on the driver side, after the series resistor.** If a
  series resistor is missing or its joint cracks, a driver-side pull-down still
  holds the input low.
- **The 6 pull-downs are the only optional parts.** Rev A may ship with them
  unpopulated, marked DNP in KiCad.

The netlist checker was committed to `tools/`, so the comparison is reproducible
rather than a result pasted from a session.

每块都是装在自己外壳里、用排线连接的独立板。

## D50A 控制口——两处更正

**一、`V` 必须接，而且是 3.3 V。** 原记录写的是 D50A 两个 `V` 脚不连接。`V` 是驱动板隔离
控制侧的供电，不接它，六个控制信号一个都不工作。`+3V3` 只从 Pi 的 1 号和 17 号针取电。
**接 5 V 会烧掉隔离输入。**

**二、这块板是 10 个连接，不是 7 个。** 原信号表漏了两个 `V` 和第二个 `G`。

| D50A 丝印 | 信号 | Pi 物理针脚 | BCM / 电源 | 通道 |
|:---:|---|---:|---|---|
| `P1` | `PWM1` | 32 | GPIO12 | 1——左 |
| `A1` | `INA1` | 16 | GPIO23 | 1 |
| `B1` | `INB1` | 18 | GPIO24 | 1 |
| `P2` | `PWM2` | 33 | GPIO13 | 2——右 |
| `A2` | `INA2` | 29 | GPIO5 | 2 |
| `B2` | `INB2` | 31 | GPIO6 | 2 |
| `G`（×2） | `GND` | 34, 39 | GND | 两个 |
| `V`（×2） | `+3V3` | **1, 17** | **3.3 V——绝不能是 5 V** | 两个 |

板上实际丝印是简写 `V P1 A1 B1 G`，不是数据手册那套 `VCC PWM1 INA1 INB1 GND`。控制口是
牛角座，所以排线两端都能防呆。

**1 号脚未解决。** 照片看不出外壳缺口朝哪一侧，也看不出有没有方形焊盘。搞错会把 `PWM1`
接到驱动板的 `P2` 输入上。它不阻塞画图，但它是**锁封装前的硬闸门**：把防呆排线插上 D50A，
再逐根量导通确认哪根线通到哪个信号。`G` 与电机电源负极 `P-` 导通，可以交叉验证。

**方向真值表**，来自 WHEELTEC 自己的 STM32 例程（`moto.c` v5.7）：

| 动作 | `A` | `B` | PWM 占空比 |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| 前进 | 高 | 低 | 3000/7200 ≈ 42% |
| 后退 | 低 | 高 | 4000/7200 ≈ 56% |

PWM 频率 10 kHz。厂家从未把两个方向脚驱动到同一电平，所以"两个都低等于停"在实测之前只是
一个假设。

## Rev A 原理图画完并核对

画了 J1（2×20）和 J2（2×5），用同名标签连接，不在图上拉长线。ERC：**30 错误 / 0 警告**，
30 条全是 2×20 排针上没用到的引脚。

随后把每条网络与信号表做**字符串比对**，不用眼睛读。**ERC 抓不出对调**——如果 `PWM1` 和
`PWM2` 贴到了对方的引脚上，两条网络各自仍有两个引脚、没有悬空，ERC 一声不响，而电机调速是反的。

## 十二个电阻没画进图

路线图和 BOM 里写着 6 个串阻和 6 个下拉，**但它们从没被画进原理图**。补上之后，每路信号被
拆成 Pi 侧和驱动侧两条网络，所以 Rev A 是 **14 个网络，不是 8 个**。

- **串阻从 0805 改成通孔，而且是必装件。** 它们在信号路径上，焊盘空着就是断路。
- **驱动侧网络按 D50A 自己的丝印命名**——`P1`、`A1`、`B1`，不叫 `PWM1_D`。网络上的名字
  就是板上的名字。
- **下拉接在驱动侧，串阻之后。** 万一串阻没装或者焊点裂了，驱动侧的下拉仍然把输入按在低电平。
- **6 个下拉是板上唯一可选的件。** Rev A 可以先不焊，在 KiCad 里标 DNP。

网表核对脚本已提交到 `tools/`，使这项比对可复现，而不是一次会话里贴出来的结果。
