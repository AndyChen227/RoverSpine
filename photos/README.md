# Photos

Organized by date and board, matching the devlog entry that references them:
`YYYY-MM-DD-<board>-<what>/`

Photograph every board **bare and assembled** before it goes on the rover. A
photo of the bare board is what lets you check a footprint or a trace later
without desoldering anything.

---

# 照片 / 中文

按日期和板子组织，和引用它们的开发日志对应：`YYYY-MM-DD-<板子>-<内容>/`

每块板在装车前都要拍**空板和焊好之后**两组照片。空板照片的作用是：以后想核对某个封装
或某根走线时，不用把元件拆下来。
